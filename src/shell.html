Campaign briefing / 3 September 1939

The peace has broken.

Karsk's armies are massing beyond the Brenner frontier. As leader of the Aster Republic, you must turn an unprepared nation into a war machine.

01 · Command the armySelect a rectangular division counter, then right-click an adjacent province. On touchscreens, use Move / attack and tap your destination.
02 · Fight a sustained warGreen bars show organization; gold bars show strength. Divisions retreat when organization breaks. Mountains and forts favor defenders.
03 · Mobilize industryAllocate military factories to rifles and tanks. Train new divisions, choose a national focus, and research better weapons.
04 · Keep supply flowingSupply travels through your territory from Asterhaven. Encircled armies weaken. Capture Karsk City and over half of Karsk's starting provinces to win.

The game starts paused. Asterhaven falling ends the campaign. This is a small original strategy prototype, not a recreation of HOI4's full feature set.
