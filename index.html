Welcome, President

Build a nation. Shape the world.

You lead Aster, the teal republic in the northwest. Time begins paused. Each simulation tick advances one week; pause whenever you need to plan.

  1. Build industry for revenue, and recruit troops to defend your borders.
  2. Balance taxes and welfare. Approval below 15% for 12 weeks ends your government.
  3. Research technology to improve income and army strength.
  4. Negotiate trade and alliances, or declare war and send troops across adjacent borders.

Win by holding 24 provinces, accumulating $3,000 with technology level 4, or forming three alliances with at least 70% approval. Lose if you lose all provinces or your government collapses.

Armies travel for two weeks. Battles cost troops on both sides. Rivals develop their territories and may declare war. Elections occur every 52 weeks.
